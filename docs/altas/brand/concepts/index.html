Stance: the myth, literally — Atlas shouldering a celestial sphere, but the titan's body is the letter A. Heavy ink weight nods to the Nous woodcut style without copying it. The blue orbital ring is the single accent. Strong at: storytelling, memorability. Weak at: very small sizes (sphere detail thins out).
