# DealerBox Brand Decisions

Status: working decisions from Omar, 2026-07-16. Product naming remains open
(see below) — do not treat the interim mark as final brand lock.

## Decided (2026-07-16)

### Interim logo: Concept 003 "Worker Badge"

- Hexagonal badge (industrial / bolt-head / *worker*) containing an isometric
  box — the literal DealerBox: an AI worker shipped in a box (likely Mac Mini).
- Chosen for: clean app icon at small sizes, simple geometry, and unity with
  the business model.
- Concepts live at `docs/altas/brand/concepts/index.html`:
  - **001 Titan Ink** — Atlas titan shouldering a celestial sphere, body forms
    the letter A; woodcut-inspired heavy ink. Keep as candidate hero artwork
    (splash screens, website) even if not the mark.
  - **002 Geometric Core** — minimal open-A monogram with floating blue
    crossbar. Keep as candidate for future redesign.
- Palette (established): dark-navy workstation aesthetic —
  navy `#060B1A`/`#0A1228`/`#101C3D`, accent blues `#4F7BE8`/`#7FA3F5`,
  paper `#F4F2EC`, ink `#0B0E14`.

### Brand separation from Hermes / Nous Research

DealerBox is building its own brand, agent, and company identity. Hermes was
the starting point; Atlas expands on it and fully rebrands. All customer
surfaces must shed Hermes/Nous branding (see
`docs/altas/rebrand/INVENTORY_CLI.md`, `INVENTORY_DESKTOP.md`). Engine
internals keep upstream names per ADR-001.

### Nous Portal: strip, then replace with DealerBox Portal

All Nous Portal integration is removed from Atlas builds (subcommand, Quick
Setup option, status lines, login defaults, diagnostics upload). The vacated
product slot is filled by the **DealerBox Portal** — see
`docs/altas/DEALERBOX_PORTAL.md`.

## Brand voice notes

- "We're not making agents. We're making **workers**." Workers do real
  dealership work: Tekion operations, employee stat tracking, emails,
  recurring reports.
- Positioning: sleek 2026 applied-AI company for dealerships.
- Every agent ships on its own box → hardware packaging is a brand surface.
